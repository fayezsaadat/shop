Measurements are body measurements, not garment measurements. Between sizes? Size up for a relaxed fit, down for compression.

How to measure

  • Chest — around the fullest part, under the arms, tape level.
  • Waist — around your natural waist, just above the hip bone.
  • Hips — around the widest part of the hips and seat.
